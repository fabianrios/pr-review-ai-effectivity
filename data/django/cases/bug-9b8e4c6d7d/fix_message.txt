Fixed #37377 -- Fixed JSONField __in crash/wrong result for Value() primitives.

Refs #36689. 9b8e4c6d7d wrapped every Value() rhs param of a JSONIn lookup
in JSON_EXTRACT()/JSON_VALUE() assuming it was already JSON-serialized.
That only holds for Value(..., JSONField()); a bare Value("primitive") gets
an inferred non-JSON output_field (e.g. CharField), so its compiled param
was a raw primitive, not JSON text.

JSONIn.resolve_expression_parameter() now JSON-encodes the Value's raw
.value itself whenever its output_field isn't JSONField.

As a side effect, this also fixes Value(True)/Value(False) in __in, which
were previously compared as raw Python bools against the JSON-extracted
lhs and never matched.

Thanks Jacob Walls for the report.

Regression in 9b8e4c6d7d47f7b2e96817ba334e9fd53c10a0df.
